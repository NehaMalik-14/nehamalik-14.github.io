Taking AI from proof of concept to everyday tooling

A closer look at the LLM-based automation framework I led at Accenture, and a small demo you can try right here.

The problem

Turning test cases into automation needed strong scripting skills, so much of the work stayed manual. The programme relied on a team of 10 manual testers.

What I built

A framework that uses large language models across the automation lifecycle, from test case generation to execution, so entry-level engineers can create and run scripts without deep programming knowledge.

I took end-to-end technical ownership and ran training sessions so teams could adopt it.

The outcome

  • 70% productivity gain
  • 10 manual testers to 3 automation testers
  • Less onboarding effort and faster script creation
  • Adopted across multiple Accenture projects
