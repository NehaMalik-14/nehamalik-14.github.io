About Me

"Great products don't just ship on time — they ship with quality built in from day one."

I'm a technology leader with nearly 12 years in software quality, test automation and AI-driven engineering for global clients in energy, healthcare, insurance and aviation — at Accenture, NTT DATA, HCL Technologies and Sopra Steria.

At Accenture I progressed from Senior Test Architect to Test Manager and AI Enablement & Automation Lead (Associate Manager) on a large-scale energy transformation programme for a leading US-based client. I architect automation frameworks, design and review Git-based CI/CD pipelines, partner with business analysts through UAT, and stay with the team through go-live.

I enjoy working alongside teams on interesting proofs of concept — most notably an LLM-based automation framework that Accenture went on to adopt across multiple projects. I'm now an MBA candidate at Durham University Business School, adding strategy and finance to my technical depth to lead technology- and AI-enabled transformation.
